# Jedi Jim's Rebel Mission: Volume 2: Return of the Light Ring

Jedi Jim is back. Darth Vexis stole the Light Ring, and Jim has to fight his way through a Star Destroyer hangar, a forest-moon speeder bike chase, a battle-station reactor core, and the Sith throne room to get it back.

Play: https://ploug76.github.io/jedi-jims-rebel-mission-vol-2/

The first game is still here, unchanged: https://ploug76.github.io/jedi-jims-rebel-mission/

- 4 missions with set pieces and boss fights: Commander Krell, the AT-ST "Ironstride", Lord Bucketon and Darth Vexis (3 phases, plus blade locks)
- Lightsaber combat: 3-hit combos, holding the saber to deflect blaster bolts back at troopers, Force push and Force jump
- Painted art, parallax, particles, dynamic lights and an original orchestral score made with WebAudio
- Controls: on phones, swipe and hold to move, swipe up to jump, and use the SABER / FORCE buttons. On a keyboard, arrows/WASD, Space, J and K. Game controllers (USB or Bluetooth) work through the Gamepad API.
- Free to play
